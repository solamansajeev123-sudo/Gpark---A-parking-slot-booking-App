About Gpark

Built for cities that don't stop moving

Gpark started with a simple frustration: circling the same block, watching the fuel gauge drop, while an open space sat two streets over the whole time. We connect drivers directly to real spaces in real time, so the search ends before it starts.

Today, thousands of drivers use Gpark every day to find, reserve, and pay for parking without ever slowing down to look for a sign.
