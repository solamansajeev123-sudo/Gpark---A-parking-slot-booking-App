About Gpark

Built for cities that don't stop moving.

Gpark started with a simple frustration: circling the same block while an open space sat just a few streets away. We connect drivers directly to live parking availability so the search ends before it starts.

Today, thousands of drivers use Gpark every day to find, reserve, and pay for parking without slowing down to look for a sign.
